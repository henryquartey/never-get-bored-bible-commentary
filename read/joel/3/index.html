NII OKAI · Joel 3:1028 November 2024

And yet in as much as this verse is a mockery call to the enemies of God and of Israel to come for battle and to arm the weak and tell them they are strong

Yes the concept of positive faith confessions is biblical and true just that the verse we have chosen to use is not so apt and appropriate.
