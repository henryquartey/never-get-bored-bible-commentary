These are dakes notes or commentary on this verse. Very informative I found it.

Time of the Events of Ruth (Ch. 1:1) One thing is clear--the events of Ruth took place in the early days of the judges, because Ruth 4:21-22 states that Boaz was the son of Salmon who married Rahab the harlot of Jericho (Mt. 1:5). If Salmon married Rahab soon after the conquest of Jericho, the event would have to occur at least during the first 50 to 75 years of the 520 years from Joshua to Saul (for the chronology of this period, see Dispensation of Law). If Boaz was born to Salmon late in life and Boaz himself married late in life, the events would still have to take place in the first 100 years of the 520 years--during the judgeship of Othniel perhaps (Judg. 3:9-11), the 18 years of servitude to Moab (Judg. 3:12-14), or at the latest, during the time of Ehud (Judg. 3:15-20). If this is true, we must acknowledge that not all the names of the men in the genealogy of Christ were given at this point, because we have only four names in the list from Salmon to the birth of David, and the lives of four men would not cover that long a period, with the usual span of life being what it was at that time. It would have required Salmon, Boaz, Obed, and Jesse to have their children in this genealogy at the average age of 125 which is very unlikely. Bible scholars agree that not all the names of royal genealogies were always given, nor were they necessary; so it seems wise to recognize that some names have been omitted (Ruth 4:20-22; Mt. 1:4-6; Lk. 3:31-32). See notes, Mt. 1:8,11.
