1Thus the heavens and the earth were finished, and all the host of them.

2And on the seventh day God ended his work which he had made; and he rested on the seventh day from all his work which he had made.

3And God blessed the seventh day, and sanctified it: because that in it he had rested from all his work which God created and made.

4These are the generations of the heavens and of the earth when they were created, in the day that the LORD God made the earth and the heavens,

NII OKAI · Genesis 2:427 October 2023

Strange hmm 🤔. So what exactly happened in Chapter 1? What was all that activity about? Was it a mere charade? God calls forth creation, makes the sky earth 🌎 sea 🌊 etc and even blesses them. And now we look at the earth in chapter 2 and there is nothing visible, because the earth 🌎 was waiting for rain ☔, which understandably is what causes stuff to grow.

Fair enough but now we see that there was no man and that is the reason given for no visible growth of anything. What exactly was going on?

This brings us to a great principle. God is a God of order, wisdom and processes. Within a simple seed is a wonderful tree 🎄 which can nest birds, feed animals etc. All that activity is hidden in a simple seed. And it is the same thing we see here. Process and order, not just random creation and calling forth of things to be.

Man, for whom all these were created, needed to be around before any of the things created matured to fullness. The caretaker of the earth needed to be present first.

Two reasons why the work which the lord had completed and rested from had not yet materialised:

1. There was not yet rain which was to trigger growth: there was a set order and principle of growth and the rain would do that job.

2. The man for whom all these things were created was not yet present to till the earth 🌎. So God formed a fully grown man 👨🏿 and planted him in the garden and caused the trees 🎄 to also grow abnormally and have fruits for the man. And from there left everything to its natural course.

NII OKAI · Genesis 2:81 January 2022

A perfect chicken 🐥 and egg 🥚 scenario. Which one did God create first? Here we see God created both.

He laid the foundation for the earth 🌎. That is, plants, animals and all productive living organisms and hid it in the seed awaiting rain ☔.

Then he goes ahead and plants a complete fully grown garden and a fully formed man 👨 to tend the garden.

9And out of the ground made the LORD God to grow every tree that is pleasant to the sight, and good for food; the tree of life also in the midst of the garden, and the tree of knowledge of good and evil.

10And a river went out of Eden to water the garden; and from thence it was parted, and became into four heads.

11The name of the first is Pison: that is it which compasseth the whole land of Havilah, where there is gold;

12And the gold of that land is good: there is bdellium and the onyx stone.

13And the name of the second river is Gihon: the same is it that compasseth the whole land of Ethiopia.

14And the name of the third river is Hiddekel: that is it which goeth toward the east of Assyria. And the fourth river is Euphrates.

15And the LORD God took the man, and put him into the garden of Eden to dress it and to keep it.

NII OKAI · Genesis 2:151 January 2022

To dress? Well understood but to keep it? Keep it from who and what?

From the onset of creation, there has been this subtle suggestion of an enemy. An enemy put in the earth who needed to be kept out. For instance in Genesis 1:28 man was commanded to subdue the earth. Why subdue if there is no rebellion or possibility of it?

28 And God blessed them, and God said unto them, Be fruitful, and multiply, and replenish the earth, and subdue it: and have dominion over the fish of the sea, and over the fowl of the air, and over every living thing that moveth upon the earth.

16And the LORD God commanded the man, saying, Of every tree of the garden thou mayest freely eat:

17But of the tree of the knowledge of good and evil, thou shalt not eat of it: for in the day that thou eatest thereof thou shalt surely die.

NII OKAI · Genesis 2:173 January 2022

The nature 🏞 of the tree could not be imparted by communicating it, though it was a tree of knowledge. Quite ironic right? How else was knowledge to be imparted? It was to be imparted by eating it. It was almost like a pen drive containing malware that is transmitted through the hardware.

See, God mentioned that the tree 🎄 held the knowledge of good and evil 😈 though evil was not in the lives of Adam and Eve. That means the desire for evil was not in them.

God could actually mention that there was good and evil and they still did not have knowledge of the distinction. They knew only good. They were good by nature 🏞.

But the tree would introduce a knowledge. The fruit 🥝 was actually one that imparted knowledge. Does that mean they did not know good either? I don't think so.

They knew good and because that was all they knew there was nothing to compare it to for them to even say that something was good and another evil.

You can only distinguish the two if you know both... For example, for most of us in Africa 🌍 who grew up in a poor community, we did not know we were poor. We just lived and knew things were hard but we were happy 😊.

Until we probably moved out of our community as we grew up and realized we were poor.

The tree 🎄 made knowledge of evil part of us. In our flesh and our souls, even our spirit. We were contaminated. This was why, the moment they ate the fruit, they saw they were naked. In the sense that they felt the shame of it.

Interestingly, God's reaction to their hiding was a strange question 🙋...

So there was found help for Adam but not comparable/meet for him. Note that animals were not eating each other and neither was man eating animals. So then the purpose of other creatures was for variety and as a support system for man. To help man 👨🏿.

But none was like him. So God went back to work, looking for help meet for him and when he finished 🏁 he brought this creature to Adam just as he had done with all the other creatures.

And there Adam himself saw that this was bone 🍖 of his and flesh of his, unlike the others. And he gave her the name Woman 👩🏽.
