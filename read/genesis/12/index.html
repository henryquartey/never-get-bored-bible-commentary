NII OKAI · Genesis 12:120 January 2022

Now the lord had said to Abraham… when did the lord say unto him? Let me check other versions.. I'll be back

I'm back, and several versions maintain the "had said" and some just used "said".

Either ways we are not told when God said to him.

However, we see earlier that Abraham's father had set off to go to the land of Canaan.

Some say he had the calling first but did not fulfil it, hence it was transferred to Abraham.

It's an inference from Genesis 11:31 KJV
And Terah took Abram his son, and Lot the son of Haran his son's son, and Sarai his daughter in law, his son Abram's wife; and they went forth with them from Ur of the Chaldees, to go into the land of Canaan; and they came unto Haran, and dwelt there.

2And I will make of thee a great nation, and I will bless thee, and make thy name great; and thou shalt be a blessing:

3And I will bless them that bless thee, and curse him that curseth thee: and in thee shall all families of the earth be blessed.

4So Abram departed, as the LORD had spoken unto him; and Lot went with him: and Abram was seventy and five years old when he departed out of Haran.

5And Abram took Sarai his wife, and Lot his brother’s son, and all their substance that they had gathered, and the souls that they had gotten in Haran; and they went forth to go into the land of Canaan; and into the land of Canaan they came.

And into the land of Canaan they came indeed.

It was as if to say that Terah had set off to go into the land of Canaan and they ended up settling in Haran but in Abraham's case he completed the journey.

NII OKAI · Genesis 12:56 January 2024

Abraham separating himself to go to Canaan was no new thought.

That was his father Terah's goal the whole time but it seems Terah could not accomplish that.

Maybe the sheer numbers of his family 👪 with other accompanying responsibilities prevented him from moving the entire tribe to a distant unknown land.

Maybe just maybe in the course of Terah's journey with his family to go to Canaan, they found a comfortable place with good land and settled.

However Abraham heeded this difficult call and gathered his family and left.

God had a plan for Abraham's lineage in Canaan and needed them to be willing to cross over from their comfort zone to Canaan, the land of promise.

And of course today we see and know how that grand plan unfolded.

6And Abram passed through the land unto the place of Sichem, unto the plain of Moreh. And the Canaanite was then in the land.

7And the LORD appeared unto Abram, and said, Unto thy seed will I give this land: and there builded he an altar unto the LORD, who appeared unto him.

NII OKAI · Genesis 12:720 January 2022

Having completed his obedience, God now appears to him to give further specifics.

Our walk with God is a journey.

As we complete one quest of obedience, he comes with the next update. In between times he nudges, watches, observes, repeats the instruction as need be till we complete the command and then fresh orders and updates come. Till then we would go in circles on the same track 🏁
