24And Abraham was ninety years old and nine, when he was circumcised in the flesh of his foreskin.

NII OKAI · Genesis 17:24

What kind of a leader was Abraham? For him to have, in the selfsame day he was instructed of God, done the circumcision of his entire household. Slave and free alike.
I make some assumptions:
His household was up to date with his GOD and very likely worshipped Jehovah.
His leadership skills and influence over his house was such that he could steer his household in line with the vision and was such a communicator that his followers caught hold of the vision and ran with it.
I know he was a skilful visionary because in his house he had highly trained servants who could take on kings in battle and come out victorious.
He was a nomad who didn’t leave things to chance.
He was a peacemaker, and abundance conscious so he could easily part ways with his own nephew giving to him the best of lands. He was both a man of practical wisdom and of faith:
when it came to the things that God had said to him, he believed it and lived in faith with God whilst living a daily practical life and preparing for the challenges of his day. I guess it's this practicality that led to the birth of Ishmael - a mistake though - and to him suggesting God bless Ishmael instead.
