Throughout scripture we see statements like the word of the lord came to someone saying.. but we are not told how… whether it is just a voice they hear or some whirlwind or apparition.

The descriptions are not given and that may be because they see nothing.

Because when men see visions of the most high we are clearly told.

No wonder God rarely appears that way.
