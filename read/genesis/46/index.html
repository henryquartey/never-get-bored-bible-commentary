NII OKAI · Genesis 46:328 January 2026

Why did God have to keep telling Jacob who he was?

I think 🤔 obviously because Jacob barely heard from God. Unlike our days where God is in our hearts, so we easily discern him.

But from the text it has been over twenty years since God’s last appearance to Jacob, so it was only right to let him know it is not some dream from some spirit, but this is him.
God almighty.

This time God didn’t even mention his signature name of God almighty but rather referenced the covenant he had with Abraham.
