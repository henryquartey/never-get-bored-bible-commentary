NII OKAI · Genesis 43:3427 January 2026

STRANGE things happening to these sons of Jacob. They knew they were in covenant with Jehovah, so these strange but good things happening to them were in their estimation the work of God Almighty.

Their fathers were idol worshippers, and so by God introducing himself as God almighty, he was etching it deep into their mind his status,

as the only true and mighty God, hence almighty. He was not one of the gods, he was God himself.

In our African traditional religion for instance we believe in smaller gods, and God himself the father of them all who is accessed via the smaller gods.

So for a god to say that he is God, there must be undoubted proof.

I’m just thinking in the light of the note on this verse, why is it that the Old Testament servants of God dwelled in peace though they lived among idol worshippers and devils,

yet the New Testament believer is jittery and constantly in fear fighting these spirits and demons, meanwhile we have a greater power, covenant and blessings.

Lord why this anomaly — I believe unwavering dedication to our GOD is the problem and difference between us and them.
