NII OKAI · Genesis 13:1818 January 2026

If indeed Abraham did all these things which he did at the word of the lord, then he is more of a priest than a nomad or a business man. I mean a man who abandons everything and carries only his family to an unknown land because his God said to do so.

And now he moves his tent because his God had told him to move through the land because it has been given to his descendants so Abraham does so.

His every move is at the whims of his God, the God of heaven. I now understand and see Abraham in a different light, he was not just a nomad or a tent dweller or business man.

That is just the aesthetics. His very life was dependent on instructions from his God.

Including even his own children from his loins and what names to give them and who to sack from his presence i.e. Ishmael and who to keep i.e. Isaac.

The God of Abraham had an agenda and he needed a willing vessel, a willing man to carry out that agenda. There are not that many willing vessels so when the gods find one they protect him and bless him greatly so his loyalty is greatly sustained because it comes with great rewards.
