NII OKAI · Genesis 35:29

History indeed repeats itself. Not too long ago, it was Isaac and Ishmael who buried father Abraham. However, this repetition is not some monotonous vicious cycle, because at the end of each man’s life he left behind much more than he came to meet and was given. So the process of nation building was under way. The quest to form national Israel was underway.
O what a quest — in the course of time and history, Israel grew and became great. And this process continued from generation to generation. All through the Old Testament.
However, in the New Testament it took on a completely different form. The nation building no longer became of the flesh, and in grafting of other nations by circumcision and the adherence to the law. No, it became by the spirit.
Interestingly, though, the common factor has and always will be this: that the process of nation building and inheritance is by birth. Strictly by birth. For both the new and the old covenants.
