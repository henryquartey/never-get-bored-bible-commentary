NII OKAI · John 11:5429 May 2023

Jesus is the son of David but he is a David's lord.

Both are true but though David is his ancestor, he is not subject to David but rather the opposite.

Jesus is our elder brother but he is not our buddy buddy brother, no he is our saviour and God.

Jesus is the son of God as we are son's of God but he is more than that, he is God.

So when the bible makes two statements which sound contrary but are both true,

There is a greater truth hidden in these seemingly contrary statements in that both are True but one truth supercedes the other in manifestation.

So for instance Jesus is David's son is true but Jesus is not subject to David as sons are to fathers. He is David's son only in ancestory but not functionally.

David rather is subject to him as his lord and God and not just as a statement but in deed and all the ways of divine worship 🛐 and subjection.
