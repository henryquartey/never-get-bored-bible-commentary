10And he said, To morrow. And he said, Be it according to thy word: that thou mayest know that there is none like unto the LORD our God.

NII OKAI · Exodus 8:117 March 2024

I’m wondering, at this time it was very likely the abuse of the Israelites had ceased — nobody wanted to be in the bad books of this their so-called God.

12And Moses and Aaron went out from Pharaoh: and Moses cried unto the LORD because of the frogs which he had brought against Pharaoh.

13And the LORD did according to the word of Moses; and the frogs died out of the houses, out of the villages, and out of the fields.

14And they gathered them together upon heaps: and the land stank.

15But when Pharaoh saw that there was respite, he hardened his heart, and hearkened not unto them; as the LORD had said.

16And the LORD said unto Moses, Say unto Aaron, Stretch out thy rod, and smite the dust of the land, that it may become lice throughout all the land of Egypt.

17And they did so; for Aaron stretched out his hand with his rod, and smote the dust of the earth, and it became lice in man, and in beast; all the dust of the land became lice throughout all the land of Egypt.

18And the magicians did so with their enchantments to bring forth lice, but they could not: so there were lice upon man, and upon beast.

19Then the magicians said unto Pharaoh, This is the finger of God: and Pharaoh’s heart was hardened, and he hearkened not unto them; as the LORD had said.

NII OKAI · Exodus 8:19

Pharaoh thought he was a demi God. He saw the finger of God yet hardened his heart. Believers see the finger of man and cower.

NII OKAI · Exodus 8:197 March 2024

It was after they could not duplicate what Moses did that they concluded that this was the finger of God, and not the curious charms of men, wise so-called.

NII OKAI · Exodus 8:198 March 2024

The Egyptians had many gods — gods of fertility, harvest etc.

I wonder who Elohim was to them, but obviously they knew that aside all the other gods there was this one God who is called Elohim who surpassed all the other gods.

Or it could mean, since they were producing results through enchantments just as Moses was, they thought they were on the same level with Moses, just that he Moses was an advanced wizard 🧙‍♂️.

However, at this point they realized that this is not the cunning and curious act of men, neither is it the power of sorcery; rather this was the finger of the Elohim (the gods), because Elohim means Gods.

NII OKAI · Exodus 8:1931 January 2026

It was only after their magic failed to work that they realised this was the finger of God. All along they thought it was a contest of power and who knew more magic.

But this time they realise it was no longer a battle of men who all had access to the realm of the spirit and to magical powers. It was now a contest of God.

God against man... suddenly it was not that someone’s magic power was more potent than the other, but rather suddenly — Moses had all the power and all of them put together had none.

Even the magicians 🧝‍♂️ concluded that this indeed was the hand 🖐️ or rather finger of God.

The great God himself who created the other gods was involved here.

The tricks and games were over.

Even though it was God who sent him, he had to make entreaties of God to reverse it.
