1And there went a man of the house of Levi, and took to wife a daughter of Levi.

2And the woman conceived, and bare a son: and when she saw him that he was a goodly child, she hid him three months.

NII OKAI · Exodus 2:230 January 2022

I don’t get it, which parent does not see the child as a goodly child 🧒.

Every parent adores their little ones. Dake’s describes him as “very fair”... Moses was indeed very beautiful even as a baby 🤱

3And when she could not longer hide him, she took for him an ark of bulrushes, and daubed it with slime and with pitch, and put the child therein; and she laid it in the flags by the river’s brink.

NII OKAI · Exodus 2:329 February 2024

This law, it indeed was in force and must have been very stringent for Moses’s parents to take this step. Does that therefore imply that, like Jesus, Moses’s age mates in Egypt had been killed?

4And his sister stood afar off, to wit what would be done to him.

NII OKAI · Exodus 2:430 January 2022

Aww.. This Miriam who watched over her junior brother and gave her life in essence.

80 years later she was to challenge him only to find out he was a senior in the spirit.

5And the daughter of Pharaoh came down to wash herself at the river; and her maidens walked along by the river’s side; and when she saw the ark among the flags, she sent her maid to fetch it.

6And when she had opened it, she saw the child: and, behold, the babe wept. And she had compassion on him, and said, This is one of the Hebrews’ children.

NII OKAI · Exodus 2:630 January 2026

She gave her son up to another for adoption.

Rather than lose him, she was willing to let him go in order to gain him.

7Then said his sister to Pharaoh’s daughter, Shall I go and call to thee a nurse of the Hebrew women, that she may nurse the child for thee?

8And Pharaoh’s daughter said to her, Go. And the maid went and called the child’s mother.

NII OKAI · Exodus 2:830 January 2026

It was a very wicked and cruel regime.

A slave-master relationship automatically existed between every Egyptian and Israelite, no matter if they were royalty or not.

It’s much like the slave trade, and all blacks were seen as being inferior.

This pattern has been etched in the sands of history from time immemorial.

9And Pharaoh’s daughter said unto her, Take this child away, and nurse it for me, and I will give thee thy wages. And the woman took the child, and nursed it.

NII OKAI · Exodus 2:930 January 2022

Oh what divine providence. How many other women hid their children 🧒.

The mother of the child becomes the nurse. Oh what love would be shown to this child 🧒,

she knowing that as soon as he is nursed, her own child will be taken away to the castle to learn the culture of kings and royalty 👑.

10And the child grew, and she brought him unto Pharaoh’s daughter, and he became her son. And she called his name Moses: and she said, Because I drew him out of the water.

NII OKAI · Exodus 2:1030 January 2022

I did not birth him, nor was he in the water of the womb, but birthed from the water 💦 of a river.

11And it came to pass in those days, when Moses was grown, that he went out unto his brethren, and looked on their burdens: and he spied an Egyptian smiting an Hebrew, one of his brethren.

NII OKAI · Exodus 2:1130 January 2022

How did he know he was Hebrew? Obviously evidence was given by his mother whiles nursing him that he was a Hebrew.

12And he looked this way and that way, and when he saw that there was no man, he slew the Egyptian, and hid him in the sand.

13And when he went out the second day, behold, two men of the Hebrews strove together: and he said to him that did the wrong, Wherefore smitest thou thy fellow?

14And he said, Who made thee a prince and a judge over us? intendest thou to kill me, as thou killedst the Egyptian? And Moses feared, and said, Surely this thing is known.

15Now when Pharaoh heard this thing, he sought to slay Moses. But Moses fled from the face of Pharaoh, and dwelt in the land of Midian: and he sat down by a well.

NII OKAI · Exodus 2:151 March 2024

This very complex turn of events is explained very simply.

The investigation that had to be carried out before concluding on Moses as guilty, the confusion in the palace about what Moses had done.

His mother’s tears 😭. Sad moments — he was going to be a fugitive forever, never to return home 🏡 and never to be seen by both his mothers.
Egypt 🇪🇬 was the superpower of the time, and if they are on the look out for you, there is no come back.

16Now the priest of Midian had seven daughters: and they came and drew water, and filled the troughs to water their father’s flock.

NII OKAI · Exodus 2:1630 January 2026

The many stories and happenings that took place by wells.

Including that of Jesus.

Well it’s understandable — wells were life, where water was drawn daily for survival.

18And when they came to Reuel their father, he said, How is it that ye are come so soon to day?

19And they said, An Egyptian delivered us out of the hand of the shepherds, and also drew water enough for us, and watered the flock.

Interestingly God’s covenant was with Abraham, Isaac and Jacob, whiles the rest by birth were participants of the covenant.

We must stand up for what we believe in even if it has been the norm or we came to meet it.

We must be bold to stand up for what we believe in.
