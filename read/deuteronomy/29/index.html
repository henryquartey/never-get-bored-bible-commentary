1These are the words of the covenant, which the LORD commanded Moses to make with the children of Israel in the land of Moab, beside the covenant which he made with them in Horeb.

2And Moses called unto all Israel, and said unto them, Ye have seen all that the LORD did before your eyes in the land of Egypt unto Pharaoh, and unto all his servants, and unto all his land;

3The great temptations which thine eyes have seen, the signs, and those great miracles:

4Yet the LORD hath not given you an heart to perceive, and eyes to see, and ears to hear, unto this day.

5And I have led you forty years in the wilderness: your clothes are not waxen old upon you, and thy shoe is not waxen old upon thy foot.

6Ye have not eaten bread, neither have ye drunk wine or strong drink: that ye might know that I am the LORD your God.

He had made a covenant with their fathers in Horeb which covenant was also binding on them.

But now he was making another with them personally. Just like the Abrahamic covenant was binding on Isaac and his son Jacob and yet God personally appeared to each of them to establish his covenant.

As I pen this down a thought came to mind that after Esau lost his birth rights, could he not have embarked on a personal journey to search for the God of his father..
He could have but did he know how to access God.

Jacob to whom belonged the covenant had God revealing Himself to him before he could personally commune with God.

Without God revealing himself they worshiped what how they knew and followed in the steps of their fathers.

But when God showed up himself things changed from impersonal to personal...

Hence their personal names of God like El bethel etc.

Back to my thought 💭. Maybe if ESAU had embarked on such a journey, just maybe God would have shown up but alas he did not do we cannot tell.
