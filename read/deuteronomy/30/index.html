NII OKAI · Deuteronomy 30:221 March 2026

the blessings of the lord and his covenant are not automatic, even in the covenant it contains blessings and curses and he treat them according to their actions.

i find the covenant of the lord with Israel are several layers deep. First he cuts covenant with father Abraham, the with his son Isaac and then Jacob... we are not told anything about joseph and moses though they were blessed.

by this time Israel is a nation. then he cuts covenants with the entire nations. the first covenant with Abraham alone seem not enough for him to impose his will and blessings upon the people.

In each generations his people must choose to activate the blessings of the covenant in order for it to work for them.

so what happens if they refuse. That would mean they have refused being blessed and no one would choose that.

Not to mentioned often they find themselves in a bad situation which only the re- enactment of the covenant can pull them out of.

we see that many times through out the history of Israel as a nation.

for the first time we see the introduction of the element of curses in the covenant.

This was only done when Israel became a nation. why curses because they were in breach of a contract. choosing to turn their back on God after he has blessed them and carried them on eagles wings to the promised land.

a land flowing with milk and honey. a land where if there were not curses God would be on the losing end of the covenant for having fulfilled his part of the deal.

So if Israel refused to worship and keep him as their God the curses are activated.
