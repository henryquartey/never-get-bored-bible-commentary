Romans 8

King James Version, with Nii Okai's journal notes · 10 notes

Read Romans 8 in the app

1There is therefore now no condemnation to them which are in Christ Jesus, who walk not after the flesh, but after the Spirit.

2For the law of the Spirit of life in Christ Jesus hath made me free from the law of sin and death.

3For what the law could not do, in that it was weak through the flesh, God sending his own Son in the likeness of sinful flesh, and for sin, condemned sin in the flesh:

4That the righteousness of the law might be fulfilled in us, who walk not after the flesh, but after the Spirit.

5For they that are after the flesh do mind the things of the flesh; but they that are after the Spirit the things of the Spirit.

6For to be carnally minded is death; but to be spiritually minded is life and peace.

7Because the carnal mind is enmity against God: for it is not subject to the law of God, neither indeed can be.

8So then they that are in the flesh cannot please God.

9But ye are not in the flesh, but in the Spirit, if so be that the Spirit of God dwell in you. Now if any man have not the Spirit of Christ, he is none of his.

10And if Christ be in you, the body is dead because of sin; but the Spirit is life because of righteousness.

NII OKAI · Romans 8:1012 May 2025

If christ is in you the body is dead because of sin, for when christ died we died with him, our bodies was crucified with him

But the spirit is life because of righteousness, for when he died he rose again by the spirit and we also rose with him and our bodies received life by the spirit unto righteousness.

This is an expansion of what I belive Paul was saying.

The next verse highlights this further.

11But if the Spirit of him that raised up Jesus from the dead dwell in you, he that raised up Christ from the dead shall also quicken your mortal bodies by his Spirit that dwelleth in you.

NII OKAI · Romans 8:116 December 2022

If the spirit of him who raised christ jesus from the dead, the body doeth no more because of sin but is made alive because of the spirit that raises the dead.

This life giving spirit gives life to our mortal bodies.

What does this life constitute?

Life: quickening - to cause to live, make alive, give life
by spiritual power to arouse and invigorate
to restore to life
to give increase of life: thus of physical life

Our mortal bodies receives quickening.

12Therefore, brethren, we are debtors, not to the flesh, to live after the flesh.

NII OKAI · Romans 8:126 December 2022

In the light of the spirit having quickened our mortal bodies we are no Longer debtors to the flesh.

13For if ye live after the flesh, ye shall die: but if ye through the Spirit do mortify the deeds of the body, ye shall live.

NII OKAI · Romans 8:136 December 2022

The are several sides and angles it the sin in the flesh issue.

By God's grace we have his quickening spirit which quickens our mortal bodies so we can live after the Spirit.

Because of this ability, we are no longer obligated to the flesh.

And because that compelling force of sin and the flesh is no longer there, we can kill and mortify sin in the flesh by the Spirit.

14For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, they are the sons of God.

15For ye have not received the spirit of bondage again to fear; but ye have received the Spirit of adoption, whereby we cry, Abba, Father.

16The Spirit itself beareth witness with our spirit, that we are the children of God:

17And if children, then heirs; heirs of God, and joint-heirs with Christ; if so be that we suffer with him, that we may be also glorified together.

18For I reckon that the sufferings of this present time are not worthy to be compared with the glory which shall be revealed in us.

19For the earnest expectation of the creature waiteth for the manifestation of the sons of God.

20For the creature was made subject to vanity, not willingly, but by reason of him who hath subjected the same in hope,

21Because the creature itself also shall be delivered from the bondage of corruption into the glorious liberty of the children of God.

22For we know that the whole creation groaneth and travaileth in pain together until now.

23And not only they, but ourselves also, which have the firstfruits of the Spirit, even we ourselves groan within ourselves, waiting for the adoption, to wit, the redemption of our body.

NII OKAI · Romans 8:236 December 2022

Hm.. Indeed, we await the redemption of thr body for though sin is death with in the body,

We groan and await the complete redemption of body where we are clothed anew with immortality in our new bodies.

24For we are saved by hope: but hope that is seen is not hope: for what a man seeth, why doth he yet hope for?

25But if we hope for that we see not, then do we with patience wait for it.

26Likewise the Spirit also helpeth our infirmities: for we know not what we should pray for as we ought: but the Spirit itself maketh intercession for us with groanings which cannot be uttered.

27And he that searcheth the hearts knoweth what is the mind of the Spirit, because he maketh intercession for the saints according to the will of God.

28And we know that all things work together for good to them that love God, to them who are the called according to his purpose.

NII OKAI · Romans 8:286 December 2022

Amen and this promise is not in isolation but against the background of groanings and spirit's cry 😢 in intercession.

29For whom he did foreknow, he also did predestinate to be conformed to the image of his Son, that he might be the firstborn among many brethren.

30Moreover whom he did predestinate, them he also called: and whom he called, them he also justified: and whom he justified, them he also glorified.

31What shall we then say to these things? If God be for us, who can be against us?

33Who shall lay any thing to the charge of God’s elect? It is God that justifieth.

34Who is he that condemneth? It is Christ that died, yea rather, that is risen again, who is even at the right hand of God, who also maketh intercession for us.
