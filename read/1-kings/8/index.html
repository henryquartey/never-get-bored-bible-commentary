Solomon in building the temple literally redesigned everything, making it bigger and more glamorous than the original tabernacle,

But the ark of covenant was not redesigned, the original one was taken from its old place and brought into the new.

Yes the room ie the most holy place itself was redesigned with cherubims standing with their wing all over the room,

But the ark itself was not touched, why?

It could be that that was the instruction from the lord, for scripture says the design of the temple David received from the lord.

But even if that was the case why Did the lord chose to keep the original ark of the covenant.

I thing the answer is found in verse...as the name suggests, it is the ark of the covenant, it symbolizes the covenant between God and Israel and so it was not to be touched, the original document ie the ark is what was kept and put in its place in the temple

In verse 21 Solomon says,

And I have set there a place for the ark, wherein is the covenant of the LORD, which he made with our fathers, when he brought them out of the land of Egypt.
