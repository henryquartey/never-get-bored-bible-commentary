1And I saw in the right hand of him that sat on the throne a book written within and on the backside, sealed with seven seals.

2And I saw a strong angel proclaiming with a loud voice, Who is worthy to open the book, and to loose the seals thereof?

3And no man in heaven, nor in earth, neither under the earth, was able to open the book, neither to look thereon.

4And I wept much, because no man was found worthy to open and to read the book, neither to look thereon.

NII OKAI · Revelation 5:87 June 2025

We have seen the elders before bowing before the throne and casting their crowns, however here the imagery we are giving is that after the book was taken by the lamp we see the elders and beasts with golden vials full of odours, and we are told those are the prayers of the saints.

Why would that be mentioned at this particular time, except there is a connection between the taking of the book by the lamp in order to break it's seals and the prayers of the saints.

And I believe that is exactly that, there is a connection, in the book contains answers to the prayers of the saints.

Prayers to the injustice, there murders and matrydom of their families, friends. The untold hardships of the persecutions they faced.

The book as we see subsequently contains the judgement of God upon the wicked.

Little wonder when earlier there was no one to open the book, that John wept. Without the seals being broken, the will continue to be further delay and the wickedness will continue.

When you genuinely serve the lord, you so wish for the coming of the lord. The world is not worthy of you.

You have no taste for the things of the world. Literally you are enduring this world and groaning constantly in this world, waiting the redemption of your body.

This is further corroborated when later in the predeeding chapters we see the saints cry for justice and they are told to wait for the rest of their brethren to be martyred like them, then when the numbers add up justice will be meted out.
