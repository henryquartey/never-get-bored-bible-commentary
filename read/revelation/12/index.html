NII OKAI · Revelation 12:111 August 2025

why would the devil accuse us before God,
except to obtain judgement against us, or to justify his persecution and abuse of the saints.

in his accusations it is stated that the saints overcame him by the blood of the lamb

which is in reference to the shed blood christ, thus justifying us and making us righteous before God,

but despite our sins and right standing with God being payed for the devil still accuses us and seek condemnation against us

so the word of our testimony comes into play and we confess that we have accepted the shared blood of the lamb and are in alignment with his finished works on the cross.

we acknowledge what has been done by Christ for us and keep to that testimony. we refuse to be condemned nor pay for what christ has already paid for.

however that is not enough for the devil, he would not budge and coninues to persecute the saints using his power and human agencies on the earth.

he causes us pain because of our acknowledgement of christ and his finished works in our lives.

he still accuses us and insists that we for we are not only not worthy but have not really received christ for real in our hearts and lives 💕 and this we refute by not loving our own lives even to the death.

we hold firm our confessions and stand our ground and our belief even unto death.

and this accusations and persecution of the devil christ forewarns us about. He says in this life we would have trouble but we must be of good cheer for he has overcome the world.
