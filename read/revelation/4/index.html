Then we had the things which are, that is the current issues pertaining to the churches and the mind of God for them and now,

Without ambiguity, the passage here says these are the thing that must be hereafter.

These 3 distinctions are so clear.

The things which are was sandwiched 🥪 between the things we was seen by John and this proclamation commencing that which must be hereafter.

NII OKAI · Revelation 4:112 December 2023

There are many interpretations that can be inferred from what 'here after meant.'

It's worth noting that when it came events of hereafter, John leaves the 🌎 earth and journeys to the heavens...

1
So firstly the visions of the son of man 👨 refers to what thou has seen

2. The visions of what is concerns the interpretation of the vision John saw and the issues of the churches.

3.
Now he is told this part of the revelation has to do with what shall be hereafter.

The question is 'here after what?'

Dakes says it's the things after the church age ie the church is raptured and not here when these things happen.

One thing is clear though John was caught up to heaven to see evens in heaven and as they affect the earth 🌎.

And these events are after the message to the church and the evens of the church ⛪.

I do not know if that meant it happened after the rapture but we see that it happens after the church has been given warnings for earth and blessing in the end of time ⌚.

And the next thing we see is the end of time activities. So then the churches time on earth 🌎 to prove their obedience is over. There is no more time to do any more works or heed any more imstructions.

It's now time for the blessings or punishment as need be.

This does not necessarily mean the church vanishes from the earth and appears in heaven.

Even though we see saints in heaven by the time John is told to Come up hither.

But those could be dead saints.

NII OKAI · Revelation 4:18 June 2025

DAKES argues that there is no mention of the church in the book of revelation from after chapter 4. Thus proving the church has been raptured to heaven.
But it is worthy nf note that the church was not mentioned as being in heaven either. The church is neither named in heaven nor earth.

Rather we see mention of saints, both in the heavens and in the earth. I think the word church is missing in heaven because by definition church is the term for ecclesia ie the called out ones.

But in heaven we are all called out, that is why we made it there in the first place. So then why is the name church not maintained in the earth?
I don’t know. Maybe that may be alluding to the church no longer being on earth but has been raptured, maybe, maybe not. So Dakes may be right but that only alludes it but doesn't provide evidence to the rapture.
