NII OKAI · Revelation 20:421 August 2025

A glimpse into eternity

Why was it possible for the devil in Rev 20:4-10 to come back and deceive the nations after a thousand years? And this was under the christ and the saints.

And I saw thrones, and they sat upon them, and judgment was given unto them: and I saw the souls of them that were beheaded for the witness of Jesus, and for the word of God, and which had not worshipped the beast, neither his image, neither had received his mark upon their foreheads, or in their hands; and they lived and reigned with Christ a thousand years. But the rest of the dead lived not again until the thousand years were finished. This is the first resurrection. Blessed and holy is he that hath part in the first resurrection: on such the second death hath no power, but they shall be priests of God and of Christ, and shall reign with him a thousand years.
And when the thousand years are expired, Satan shall be loosed out of his prison, And shall go out to deceive the nations which are in the four quarters of the earth, Gog and Magog, to gather them together to battle: the number of whom is as the sand of the sea. And they went up on the breadth of the earth, and compassed the camp of the saints about, and the beloved city: and fire came down from God out of heaven, and devoured them. And the devil that deceived them was cast into the lake of fire and brimstone, where the beast and the false prophet are, and shall be tormented day and night for ever and ever

The question I ask is this, what kind of system was being run that allowed such rebellion even to the point of the devil amassing an army for himself. Only for that army and the devil to be destroyed once and for all with a evil men.

I think it is a similar system in a sense that God is running now, where we live in a world where men call the shots. We have free will and freely exercise it.

It seems evil is running amok perpetually but in the scheme of God this will come to an end. We are flesh and mortal so this evil world seem to go on and on but to God is it but just a few moment in time and it will all be over.

So also in the reign of christ and the saints we see the reign a thousand years. Yes wow a thousand years is a long time but it is mentioned in just a single verse as if it is nothing.

And during those thousand years there will be order and righteousness and the goodness of the lord but men will still be free willed.

That is why after the thousand years when the devil is let lose one more time, he is able to deceive the nations again.

This shows in the rule of God and his christ men could still choose to rebel. And they did when the opportunity came.

So it is not truly that God will wave some magic wand and All men will be righteous and holy and love him.

No not at all just that under his reign, righteousness and justice will be the order of the day.

Until once more the devil is let lose and he begins his deception. I just wonder will the men of that time know about the prophecy in the book of revelations.

Will they know that they are just part of a play book and they devil himself is also within that play book. And that the words of the devil and the seeming liberty with which he is running amok and gathering the nations is only being allowed by God? He the devil is really not powerful and independent as he postulates.

That he has actually been allowed and this allowance was prophesied thousands of years ago. Will men even be aware of that prophesy for them to resist the devil.

This makes me wonder, our world we live in today, could it not be the same thing that is going on.

That we are all just part of God’s play book that was prophesied to the word that then was and we that are alive today are just acting players in the book.

And the devil is only doing so much as he is allowed to. We know the story of the future of our world through prophecies like revelations yet the past of our world is shrouded in mystery and shadows. So many unanswered questions and it seems we just dropped in the middle of nowhere with no sense of history and of the past to know exactly what is going on.

They are some hints here and there but mostly it is just theories.

I believe we are part of a prophesy that was given to a world that lived and now we looking into the future see another world coming and what their own play book will be like but they will not have that awareness like we do. We are looking into the future and will be alive to witness it but they will be looking at the past and what will be at that time their present.

However, the spirit of God will continue to give wisdom and prompt men of the future so men are kept in the ways of righteousness just as he does in our world today.
