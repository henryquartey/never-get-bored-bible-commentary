Proverbs 21

King James Version, with Nii Okai's journal notes · 13 notes

Read Proverbs 21 in the app

1The king’s heart is in the hand of the LORD, as the rivers of water: he turneth it whithersoever he will.

2Every way of a man is right in his own eyes: but the LORD pondereth the hearts.

3To do justice and judgment is more acceptable to the LORD than sacrifice.

4An high look, and a proud heart, and the plowing of the wicked, is sin.

5The thoughts of the diligent tend only to plenteousness; but of every one that is hasty only to want.

NII OKAI · Proverbs 21:529 July 2024

Because he will follow up on his thoughts 💭 with hard work so he will reap plenty,

The hasty will not take time to do what is right and yet desire results.

They will have nothing but only want and hunger.

6The getting of treasures by a lying tongue is a vanity tossed to and fro of them that seek death.

NII OKAI · Proverbs 21:68 June 2022

That's quiet à harsh consequences of ill gotten wealth.

But it does happen especially death in a figurative sense.

7The robbery of the wicked shall destroy them; because they refuse to do judgment.

8The way of man is froward and strange: but as for the pure, his work is right.

9It is better to dwell in a corner of the housetop, than with a brawling woman in a wide house.

10The soul of the wicked desireth evil: his neighbour findeth no favour in his eyes.

11When the scorner is punished, the simple is made wise: and when the wise is instructed, he receiveth knowledge.

NII OKAI · Proverbs 21:1129 July 2024

For the scorner, it takes punishment for his to stop his scorning,

For the simple seeing such brute punishment on the scorner now teaches him wisdom.

As for the wise, words alone or are more than enough to set him on the right course and give him knowledge.

12The righteous man wisely considereth the house of the wicked: but God overthroweth the wicked for their wickedness.

13Whoso stoppeth his ears at the cry of the poor, he also shall cry himself, but shall not be heard.

NII OKAI · Proverbs 21:138 June 2022

This does not mean senselessly giving to the poor without doing some due diligence when you can or asking and pondering to ensure it is a genuine need.

14A gift in secret pacifieth anger: and a reward in the bosom strong wrath.

NII OKAI · Proverbs 21:148 June 2022

Though anger and wrath are emotions which are intangibles, there are tangible ways to pacify it.

15It is joy to the just to do judgment: but destruction shall be to the workers of iniquity.

NII OKAI · Proverbs 21:1529 July 2024

To the just it is joy to met out justice,

It is the workers of 👷 iniquity however who shall suffer.

16The man that wandereth out of the way of understanding shall remain in the congregation of the dead.

NII OKAI · Proverbs 21:168 June 2022

This goes to show how costly lack of understanding can be betimes.

It can indeed lead to death of family 👪 property etc etc.

17He that loveth pleasure shall be a poor man: he that loveth wine and oil shall not be rich.

NII OKAI · Proverbs 21:178 June 2022

And there à so many people with much in hand but have not made ends meet because of lifestyle.

18The wicked shall be a ransom for the righteous, and the transgressor for the upright.

NII OKAI · Proverbs 21:1829 July 2024

But such injustice was metted out to the lord when the jews, the very custodian of the law of God and such wisdom did the very opposite.

19It is better to dwell in the wilderness, than with a contentious and an angry woman.

20There is treasure to be desired and oil in the dwelling of the wise; but a foolish man spendeth it up.

21He that followeth after righteousness and mercy findeth life, righteousness, and honour.

22A wise man scaleth the city of the mighty, and casteth down the strength of the confidence thereof.

23Whoso keepeth his mouth and his tongue keepeth his soul from troubles.

24Proud and haughty scorner is his name, who dealeth in proud wrath.

25The desire of the slothful killeth him; for his hands refuse to labour.

NII OKAI · Proverbs 21:2529 July 2024

Yes and literally this is what happens, the slothful suffer want and eventually die no matter how long it take in a state of need and lack.

26He coveteth greedily all the day long: but the righteous giveth and spareth not.

27The sacrifice of the wicked is abomination: how much more, when he bringeth it with a wicked mind?

NII OKAI · Proverbs 21:2729 July 2024

Is it a wicked mind as in to win the favour and blessing of the lord in his evil endeavors?

I guess that is the case, trusting that God somehow would give blessings to his wicked ways.

28A false witness shall perish: but the man that heareth speaketh constantly.

29A wicked man hardeneth his face: but as for the upright, he directeth his way.

NII OKAI · Proverbs 21:298 June 2022

You see a hardened face, haughty temperament, winking eyes 👀,

You be dealing with a wicked man 👨.

30There is no wisdom nor understanding nor counsel against the LORD.

NII OKAI · Proverbs 21:308 June 2022

Amen

31The horse is prepared against the day of battle: but safety is of the LORD.
