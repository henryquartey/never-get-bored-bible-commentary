9Now the pit wherein Ishmael had cast all the dead bodies of the men, whom he had slain because of Gedaliah, was it which Asa the king had made for fear of Baasha king of Israel: and Ishmael the son of Nethaniah filled it with them that were slain.

NII OKAI · Jeremiah 41:931 August 2026

Why was this being described, was it that it came to fulfil a certain prophecy or it was just an observation. Google explains it was a historical observation - underground water cistern large enough to hold so many bodies goes to show how vast it was. It was supposed to hold water for the people in times of wars where sometimes siege prevented food and water from coming into walled cities.
