About Matthew 3

John the Baptist preaches repentance in the wilderness of Judaea. Jesus comes to be baptised by him, the Spirit descends like a dove, and a voice says "This is my beloved Son". Also in Mark 1 and Luke 3.

Written by: Not named — the early church says Matthew (Levi), the tax collector · About AD 50–70

1In those days came John the Baptist, preaching in the wilderness of Judaea,

2And saying, Repent ye: for the kingdom of heaven is at hand.

3For this is he that was spoken of by the prophet Esaias, saying, The voice of one crying in the wilderness, Prepare ye the way of the Lord, make his paths straight.

NII OKAI’S NOTE · Matthew 3:323 January 2025

Prophecy prophecy prophecy, I searched where exactly in Isaiah and the Old Testament that this was written, and it says:

Isaiah 40:3-5
The voice of him that crieth in the wilderness, Prepare ye the way of the LORD,
make straight in the desert a highway for our God.
4 Every valley shall be exalted,
and every mountain and hill shall be made low:
and the crooked shall be made straight,
and the rough places plain:
5 And the glory of the LORD shall be revealed,
and all flesh shall see it together:
for the mouth of the LORD hath spoken it.

The Dake's commentary provides an additional scripture in Malachi 3:1:

Malachi 3:1
Behold, I will send my messenger, and he shall prepare the way before me:
and the Lord, whom ye seek, shall suddenly come to his temple,
even the messenger of the covenant, whom ye delight in:
behold, he shall come, saith the LORD of hosts.

What amazes me is that in just this one verse is a period of over 2000 years.

2000 years between “Behold, I will send my messenger” and “the Lord whom you seek shall suddenly come to his temple”,

talking of the end times when Christ appears.

This makes me realise that the books of prophecy, for example Revelation, might have many events happen not written down, or written down but miles apart in their time zone, except where the time is explicitly given.

But even where timing of events are given, there might be gaps of time not made mention of.

We have to be very humble and sober whenever we try to predict future events using scripture.

Our firm assurance is that in the fullness of time all things shall align as written.

For there shall not one jot of the scripture be left unfulfilled.

Heaven and earth shall pass away, but God's word abides forever.

4And the same John had his raiment of camel’s hair, and a leathern girdle about his loins; and his meat was locusts and wild honey.

5Then went out to him Jerusalem, and all Judaea, and all the region round about Jordan,

6And were baptized of him in Jordan, confessing their sins.

7But when he saw many of the Pharisees and Sadducees come to his baptism, he said unto them, O generation of vipers, who hath warned you to flee from the wrath to come?

NII OKAI’S NOTE · Matthew 3:79 November 2026

The coming wrath was the kingdom of GOD, a kingdom of righteousness and holiness.

These Pharisees who were supposed to be the religious leaders of the time and presumably to be the first or among the first to enter the promised kingdom 👑knew their secret deeds and wickedness.

So now they came to be baptized if per chance they could secure their place in the kingdom.

John saw 👀right through their hypocrisy and reprove them.

Even John himself did not fully understand the nature and timing ⏳of his message.

The kingdom was at hand 🖐️and indeed it was here but not as a conquering kingdom but as one that is preached and lived.

Nobody knew the time lines. Not even the Son, only GOD knew and justifiably so.

Who would kill himself for a 2 millennia plus vision? If the early church ⛪ had known it would have slowed them down. So this knowledge has remained secret until its time of fulfilment.

The has always been thus sense of haste and pending judgement that is to come with GOD's kingdom which has sped its growth and outreach.

8Bring forth therefore fruits meet for repentance:

9And think not to say within yourselves, We have Abraham to our father: for I say unto you, that God is able of these stones to raise up children unto Abraham.

10And now also the axe is laid unto the root of the trees: therefore every tree which bringeth not forth good fruit is hewn down, and cast into the fire.

11I indeed baptize you with water unto repentance: but he that cometh after me is mightier than I, whose shoes I am not worthy to bear: he shall baptize you with the Holy Ghost, and with fire:

NII OKAI’S NOTE · Matthew 3:114 October 2022

Things had changed, there seems to be something pending.
Which prophet comes preaching about another prophet who was to come after him?

A shift had happened, and though he baptized, he knew it was in preparation for the one.

These were strange times indeed.

Again, later they sent to ask if he was the Messiah, to which he denied. Where did such rumors gain grounds?

Likely because 30 years back rumors about this wonder child had been spreading and gradually gaining grounds.

The events of the magi, the shepherds, though long ago, had gained some traction.

Aside the fact that there was a general expectation of the Messiah as prophesied in Daniel.

NII OKAI’S NOTE · Matthew 3:1512 January 2023

Men of the Spirit, men of wisdom. Anybody standing by and listening 👂 would wonder what these two ✌️ are talking about.

John is baptising, he sees Jesus and says, no, I can't baptise you, you baptise me.

And they seem to understand why John would make an unusual request.

This was John's territory and he was the Baptist, so why would he make such requests — a bystander would ask.

Now things get weirder when Jesus responds like, permit it to be so for now, you know we have an arrangement from the dateless past.

Having said that, there was no more arguing.

John goes ahead and baptises him. What were these two talking about? They've never met, so what fulfillment of all righteousness was that?

The way of the Spirit is the way of wisdom.

16And Jesus, when he was baptized, went up straightway out of the water: and, lo, the heavens were opened unto him, and he saw the Spirit of God descending like a dove, and lighting upon him:

Could it be that the first time was by discernment of prophetic vision and then the actual thing did happen?
