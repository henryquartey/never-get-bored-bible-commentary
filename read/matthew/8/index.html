Yes there were miracle, mNy great miracles, yes there were signs and wonders and the for taste if the kingdom but there were also many great trials that can cause one to be offended, wounded ans to fall off... Jesus was forewarning him

NII OKAI · Matthew 8:254 February 2025

Who does that, who cries to another man to save them.

Normally men in such a situation of life and death cry out to God or their gods as was the case of jonah.

No man goes crying to another man for salvation in such matters.

In fact when you cry out to God in such instances, you are pleading for mercy knowing very well that he may or may not save you.

But here was a man - God in the flesh who by all means will save when cried out to.

It was not a matter of he may or may not. It was a matter of he will save them.

Oh such surety to have God in the flesh - Jesus christ.

And when he left he left us a promise that as sure as he was with us and we could count on him even in such dire times, he was sending us another like himself - even the holy spirit.

So why then are we not experiencing the holy spirit like the way the disciples experienced jesus.

One of the reasons is that he is spirit and not flesh - and that is our limitation - relating to spirit.

We fail many times in such respect, many many times the flesh excels than the spirit.

And even when the spirit forwarns us or guides us we are negligent and thus unable to yield.

I think that is the main deal and limitation - for instance jesus at every instance could heal any sickness and disease using difference techniques, how does he know what to do in each situation-

By the guidance of the holy spirit. For some he laid hands, others he asked to go wash in a pool and yet others he just spoke.

By knowing the mind and will of the spirit he is able to produce results.

I will not be presumptuous to think that is the only reason for our failure to walk in constant power and victory but I believe that is a major reason.
