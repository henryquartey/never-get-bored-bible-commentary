1And again he entered into Capernaum after some days; and it was noised that he was in the house.

2And straightway many were gathered together, insomuch that there was no room to receive them, no, not so much as about the door: and he preached the word unto them.

3And they come unto him, bringing one sick of the palsy, which was borne of four.

4And when they could not come nigh unto him for the press, they uncovered the roof where he was: and when they had broken it up, they let down the bed wherein the sick of the palsy lay.

5When Jesus saw their faith, he said unto the sick of the palsy, Son, thy sins be forgiven thee.

NII OKAI · Mark 2:51 February 2025

We have exhibited such audacious faith and yet our sick have not been made well.

They have rather gotten worse and worse beyond remedy and died.

And we were left wondering if the word of God was true.

Instead, of wondering at the men who claimed to represent what the word of God stood for

We wondered at the wrong thing, our wonder should have been if the so called men of God and disciples of christ were true.

God's word was and remains true, the men who claim to be sent from him were false.

False in that they feigned having his power when they did not.

6But there were certain of the scribes sitting there, and reasoning in their hearts,

7Why doth this man thus speak blasphemies? who can forgive sins but God only?

NII OKAI · Mark 2:726 January 2023

That was their thinking.

So for Jesus to have publicly said that he was forgiving the. Man's sin,

Was alluding to his deity.

8And immediately when Jesus perceived in his spirit that they so reasoned within themselves, he said unto them, Why reason ye these things in your hearts?

9Whether is it easier to say to the sick of the palsy, Thy sins be forgiven thee; or to say, Arise, and take up thy bed, and walk?

10But that ye may know that the Son of man hath power on earth to forgive sins,( he saith to the sick of the palsy,)

11I say unto thee, Arise, and take up thy bed, and go thy way into thine house.

12And immediately he arose, took up the bed, and went forth before them all; insomuch that they were all amazed, and glorified God, saying, We never saw it on this fashion.

NII OKAI · Mark 2:121 February 2025

Indeed, they had never seen power exercised in such fashion.

All they knew prior were they teachings of the pharisees,

Words without power.

Today, we have the same teachings of men of God, words without power. Worse than that is words with feigned power,
Wolves in sheep clothing who devour the flock, and use their wool for clothing, they take the milk for themselves and eat the young ones as well...

I can imagine Peter and Co coming to our world today, to see that some of the richest people in a country are not Christians but the pastors who lead them.

They take from them their money in the name of God to amass rich for themselves,
they devour the flock by sleeping with the members and those who refused are raped and gaged or ostracised.

They're is an anomaly in the body and among the called.

Something is amiss.

Peter and Co would not recognise Christianity if they were to come leave in our times.

The way Jesus calls people.. Simply follow me and that's that.

15And it came to pass, that, as Jesus sat at meat in his house, many publicans and sinners sat also together with Jesus and his disciples: for there were many, and they followed him.

16And when the scribes and Pharisees saw him eat with publicans and sinners, they said unto his disciples, How is it that he eateth and drinketh with publicans and sinners?

17When Jesus heard it, he saith unto them, They that are whole have no need of the physician, but they that are sick: I came not to call the righteous, but sinners to repentance.

NII OKAI · Mark 2:177 February 2025

this was not to say the pharisees were righteous but they SELF claimed to be and so playing along with them Jesus's made them understand that they needed not him nor his company.

he knew their evil heart and ways.

18And the disciples of John and of the Pharisees used to fast: and they come and say unto him, Why do the disciples of John and of the Pharisees fast, but thy disciples fast not?

19And Jesus said unto them, Can the children of the bridechamber fast, while the bridegroom is with them? as long as they have the bridegroom with them, they can not fast.

20But the days will come, when the bridegroom shall be taken away from them, and then shall they fast in those days.

21No man also seweth a piece of new cloth on an old garment: else the new piece that filled it up taketh away from the old, and the rent is made worse.

22And no man putteth new wine into old bottles: else the new wine doth burst the bottles, and the wine is spilled, and the bottles will be marred: but new wine must be put into new bottles.

NII OKAI · Mark 2:2227 January 2023

In the day's when the bridegroom is taken away, they will fast.

But for now the bride groom 🤵 is here so they celebrate 🎉 and make merry.

These my disciples are like old wine 🍷 skins.
They must be renewed to contain the new wine of Christianity which I'll pour out on them.

For now their happy go lucky selves must under go changes to meet the futur responsibility of Christianity.

Just as you will not sow and new cloth on an old less you injure the old and the tear is made worse so my disciples are undergoing the requisite training.

Even after such times of Power and glory with christ they went back to fishing when he died.

They were still not ready, till after his resurrection Jesus had to show himself to them bodily.

Even then they still did not know what to do, till Jesus came back to their hearts 💕 and entered their spirit in the person of the holy spirit.

Then they knew what to do and could actually do it.

The holy spirit is all we need.

NII OKAI · Mark 2:227 October 2022

The new and the old covenants cannot mix up. The old must go for the new to come.

23And it came to pass, that he went through the corn fields on the sabbath day; and his disciples began, as they went, to pluck the ears of corn.

24And the Pharisees said unto him, Behold, why do they on the sabbath day that which is not lawful?

25And he said unto them, Have ye never read what David did, when he had need, and was an hungred, he, and they that were with him?

26How he went into the house of God in the days of Abiathar the high priest, and did eat the shewbread, which is not lawful to eat but for the priests, and gave also to them which were with him?

27And he said unto them, The sabbath was made for man, and not man for the sabbath:

Jesus knew his exact mission and what he was here to do.

He knew what would be abolished in his death and what new thing would be established from his rising forth.
