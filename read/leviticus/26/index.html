1Ye shall make you no idols nor graven image, neither rear you up a standing image, neither shall ye set up any image of stone in your land, to bow down unto it: for I am the LORD your God.

2Ye shall keep my sabbaths, and reverence my sanctuary: I am the LORD.

3If ye walk in my statutes, and keep my commandments, and do them;

4Then I will give you rain in due season, and the land shall yield her increase, and the trees of the field shall yield their fruit.

5And your threshing shall reach unto the vintage, and the vintage shall reach unto the sowing time: and ye shall eat your bread to the full, and dwell in your land safely.

6And I will give peace in the land, and ye shall lie down, and none shall make you afraid: and I will rid evil beasts out of the land, neither shall the sword go through your land.

7And ye shall chase your enemies, and they shall fall before you by the sword.

NII OKAI · Leviticus 26:149 April 2024

What is the difference between commands, statutes and judgements?

Judgements are easier, in that they are God's decisions concerning issues and cases that come up, especially from breaking any of his laws.

So all these burnt offerings, stoning offenders, etc. are his judgements concerning breaking his laws.

I presume then that his statutes are the same as his laws, whiles his commandments may be like decrees.

I would have to search out the original Hebrew words.

NII OKAI · Leviticus 26:4024 February 2026

When I read this passage, I wondered why the repentance of the people attracted the Lord's attention and mercy, meanwhile in the preceding verses he himself said he will not accept their incense or offering.

What had changed this time? It is because the first time the people just continued in their ways and sins, business as usual, and yet still maintained a posture of religiosity and holiness.

This is what the verse says:

“And I will make your cities waste, and bring your sanctuaries unto desolation, and I will not smell the savour of your sweet odours.”
Leviticus 26:31 KJV

So these people were offering sweet odours to the Lord as if all was well. Meanwhile they were disobeying his laws.

They kept the rituals but not his laws and judgements.

However, when finally they came to themselves and recognized their wicked ways and humbled themselves, God began their redemption.

He further states that they have to accept the punishment of their iniquity, in that they deserved it and it was just.

Wow.. complete repentance... I have been there before and I'm there even now. Lord, help me... Help us all.

I think, like Daniel, one can repent on his own behalf and that of his nation, family, etc.

Lord, have mercy on my family.
