Read Hebrews 13 in the app

1Let brotherly love continue.

2Be not forgetful to entertain strangers: for there by some have entertained angels unawares.

3Remember them that are in bonds, as bound with them; and them which suffer adversity, as being yourselves also in the body.

4Marriage is honourable in all, and the bed undefiled: but whoremongers and adulterers God will judge.

5Let your conversation be without covetousness; and be content with such things as ye have: for he hath said, I will never leave thee, nor forsake thee.

6So that we may boldly say, The Lord is my helper, and I will not fear what man shall do unto me.

7Remember them which have the rule over you, who have spoken unto you the word of God: whose faith follow, considering the end of their conversation.

8Jesus Christ the same yesterday, and to day, and for ever.

9Be not carried about with divers and strange doctrines. For it is a good thing that the heart be established with grace; not with meats, which have not profited them that have been occupied therein.

NII OKAI · Hebrews 13:923 May 2025

This verse and others point to why many believe Hebrews was written by Paul...

He peculiarly talked of meats not defiling us and of righteousness by faith alone.

And in preceding verses he talks of his bonds.. All this was peculiar to him in his writings.

10We have an altar, where of they have no right to eat which serve the tabernacle.

11For the bodies of those beasts, whose blood is brought into the sanctuary by the high priest for sin, are burned without the camp.

12Wherefore Jesus also, that he might sanctify the people with his own blood, suffered without the gate.

13Let us go forth therefore unto him without the camp, bearing his reproach.

14For here have we no continuing city, but we seek one to come.

NII OKAI · Hebrews 13:1521 December 2022

Not blood animals but the fruit 🥝 of our lips....

It looks simple and not powerful but that is the believers power and "enchantement" before God.

Not some blood or oil or fleshly symbol but faith in obedience.

It's faithlessness in the simple provisions that God has given in his word that make us go for excess and symbols and tokens.

16But to do good and to communicate forget not: for with such sacrifices God is well pleased.

18Pray for us: for we trust we have a good conscience, in all things willing to live honestly.
