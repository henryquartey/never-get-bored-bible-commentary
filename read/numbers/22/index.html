1And the children of Israel set forward, and pitched in the plains of Moab on this side Jordan by Jericho.

2And Balak the son of Zippor saw all that Israel had done to the Amorites.

3And Moab was sore afraid of the people, because they were many: and Moab was distressed because of the children of Israel.

4And Moab said unto the elders of Midian, Now shall this company lick up all that are round about us, as the ox licketh up the grass of the field. And Balak the son of Zippor was king of the Moabites at that time.

5He sent messengers therefore unto Balaam the son of Beor to Pethor, which is by the river of the land of the children of his people, to call him, saying, Behold, there is a people come out from Egypt: behold, they cover the face of the earth, and they abide over against me:

6Come now therefore, I pray thee, curse me this people; for they are too mighty for me: peradventure I shall prevail, that we may smite them, and that I may drive them out of the land: for I wot that he whom thou blessest is blessed, and he whom thou cursest is cursed.

7And the elders of Moab and the elders of Midian departed with the rewards of divination in their hand; and they came unto Balaam, and spake unto him the words of Balak.

9And God came unto Balaam, and said, What men are these with thee?

NII OKAI · Numbers 22:918 December 2023

Balaam consulted with God. Balaam was not of Israel, yet he knew the Lord.

He was a revered and feared 😱 man of God, or as the heathen understood it, one whose curses 🤬 and blessings 🙌 came to pass.

Bear in mind that the book of Numbers was written by Moses — Moses the great prophet and proponent of the Law and the Old Testament.

This Moses, great and mighty prince 🤴 in Israel 🇮🇱, was here writing about another,

another man of God who sought to resist Israel 🇮🇱.

A kingdom 👑 divided against itself shall not stand, and so God hindered Balaam.

But it is interesting to note that these two great men of God lived and encountered each other in this dispensation.

There are some who say he was not of God.

I beg to differ. All the nations of the promised land were pagans and consulted idols and divined 🔮 perpetually.

With which of them did the Lord consult before doing anything? None on record. Do you think the occult powers of these nations did not curse Israel 🇮🇱?

Of course they did.

But now here comes a man coming to curse a people from Egypt 🇪🇬 in the name of the Lord, and the Lord had to intervene.

Why do such, if Balaam was no man of God?

Behold, there is a people come out of Egypt, which covereth the face of the earth: come now, curse me them; peradventure I shall be able to overcome them, and drive them out.

10And Balaam said unto God, Balak the son of Zippor, king of Moab, hath sent unto me, saying,

11Behold, there is a people come out of Egypt, which covereth the face of the earth: come now, curse me them; peradventure I shall be able to overcome them, and drive them out.

12And God said unto Balaam, Thou shalt not go with them; thou shalt not curse the people: for they are blessed.

NII OKAI · Numbers 22:124 March 2022

By implication, if you are not blessed 🌾, then you deserve, or at least qualify for, a curse.

It's akin to “he that hath, more will be added”, but he that hath not a blessing, even a curse will be given him.

Life is so not fair: to the blessed, a curse passes over him and he gets more blessed, or at least exempted from the curse,

but to the ordinary man who has not, even the little is taken away.

How sad 😔. This life.

Matthew 13:12 KJV
For whosoever hath, to him shall be given, and he shall have more abundance: but whosoever hath not, from him shall be taken away even that he hath.

NII OKAI · Numbers 22:1218 December 2023

A blessed people must not be cursed 🤬.

It is an abomination, and not permitted nor proper, for a blessed person to be cursed 🤬.

That does not mean it cannot happen, but it is not permitted.

It is hindered, and so we see that as Balak attempted to, he was hindered; he was not permitted.

15And Balak sent yet again princes, more, and more honourable than they.

16And they came to Balaam, and said to him, Thus saith Balak the son of Zippor, Let nothing, I pray thee, hinder thee from coming unto me:

17For I will promote thee unto very great honour, and I will do whatsoever thou sayest unto me: come therefore, I pray thee, curse me this people.

18And Balaam answered and said unto the servants of Balak, If Balak would give me his house full of silver and gold, I cannot go beyond the word of the LORD my God, to do less or more.

NII OKAI · Numbers 22:184 March 2022

He knew that the realm of the spirit was not subject to his beck and call.

NII OKAI · Numbers 22:185 March 2026

When and how did Balaam gain the reputation of one who is able to curse and one who is able to bless?

Yes, we all do know that prophets carry such power, but Balaam seems to be specially noted for possessing such abilities. This is not a typical way prophets of God are described.

And especially for a heathen nation and king to think of buying for his perusal such abilities — it casts a lot of shadow on the reputation of Balaam.

Some say he was a soothsayer and diviner, and others a prophet of God who was just ambitious for money. Either way, he had a relationship with God, and his source of prophecy and modus operandi relied on the God of Israel.

19Now therefore, I pray you, tarry ye also here this night, that I may know what the LORD will say unto me more.

20And God came unto Balaam at night, and said unto him, If the men come to call thee, rise up, and go with them; but yet the word which I shall say unto thee, that shalt thou do.

21And Balaam rose up in the morning, and saddled his ass, and went with the princes of Moab.

22And God’s anger was kindled because he went: and the angel of the LORD stood in the way for an adversary against him. Now he was riding upon his ass, and his two servants were with him.

Ah, I don't get it — was it not God who gave the approval?

NII OKAI · Numbers 22:286 March 2022

🤔 Is this the garden of Eden? Is that a talking animal, like the talking snake 🐍 of Eden?

You know many have suggested that Eden's events were just figurative. Fair enough, we know animals don't talk and can't reason logically.

But is that really true? The fact that they don't think in a complex manner does not mean they can't reason at all, nor talk logically if they could.

Think 🤔 about this: if your dog 🐶 could talk, what would it say when hungry, or when a stranger comes to your compound?

Would it not say, I am hungry, or I smell someone I don't know?

How difficult is that? We have either lost our ability to hear them talk, or, as illustrated here, they have lost the ability to talk. Rather they gesture.

Could it be that there was much communication in Eden?

Oh, one can imagine after the fall, and much to the shock of Adam, that all the animals came out mute 🔕.

He asks for something and they can't understand nor talk back.

Today we train dogs to go pick sticks, balls, search luggage etc. And they do this pretty intelligently.

Something in the fall just deprived them of the talking ability.
