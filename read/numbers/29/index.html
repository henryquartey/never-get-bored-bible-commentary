1And in the seventh month, on the first day of the month, ye shall have an holy convocation; ye shall do no servile work: it is a day of blowing the trumpets unto you.

2And ye shall offer a burnt offering for a sweet savour unto the LORD; one young bullock, one ram, and seven lambs of the first year without blemish:

3And their meat offering shall be of flour mingled with oil, three tenth deals for a bullock, and two tenth deals for a ram,

4And one tenth deal for one lamb, throughout the seven lambs:

5And one kid of the goats for a sin offering, to make an atonement for you:

6Beside the burnt offering of the month, and his meat offering, and the daily burnt offering, and his meat offering, and their drink offerings, according unto their manner, for a sweet savour, a sacrifice made by fire unto the LORD.

NII OKAI · Numbers 29:68 March 2026

All these offerings meant the house of God was very wealthy and well taken care of, meaning the Levites also were living very well. This goes to show the mind of God for his people, how God intended to bless his people.

To bless them so much that his house was also well taken care of. That was his mind, not to impoverish the congregation whiles his house gets blessed.

7And ye shall have on the tenth day of this seventh month an holy convocation; and ye shall afflict your souls: ye shall not do any work therein:

NII OKAI · Numbers 29:409 March 2022

I can but only imagine the bloodshed and continuous oblation to the Lord.

That was the cost to endure man dwelt with sinful flesh.

Sin always had to be atoned for.

This ordinance was carefully thought 💭 of and crafted by God and the spirits and the acceptable institution to show the ills of sin and the need for atonement to be made only through such gruesome butchery.

Continual burnt 🔥 offerings and crying of clean beasts to make atonement. That was the only acceptable sacrifice until the perfect sacrifice of Christ was made.
