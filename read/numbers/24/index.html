NII OKAI · Numbers 24:17 March 2026

Balaam obviously worked with God; that was why he was so successful.

But I wonder why he was said to have gone to seek for enchantment, rather than say he had gone to seek the Lord in prayer, or he went to seek the face of God, or cry out to God — but rather what he did was described as enchantment.

What he did was described this way because he was not seeking the will of God; he was seeking how to change the will of God, and that was why what he did was called enchantments.

Think of sorcerers, witches and co. Their craft is described as enchantments because they seek their own will. They seek how to cause what they desire to happen, regardless of whether it was the will of God or not.

That is why it is called enchantment, and that is why God hates it so much. It is about bending nature and compelling the supernatural to act on the natural to please one's desires.

God won't do what is contrary to himself, but the devils will. They will do what man desires in exchange for something they want.

But God will not do what man wants if it contradicts his nature and purpose.

Same way, the devils will not do what will counter their will and purpose; but the devils are not God, so they are usurping authority in rebellion by causing man to seek after enchantments.

In the long run the devils destroy and cause harm, but God blesses.

The devil will give you what you desire, seemingly, but it is deception, to end up destroying you, for he is a thief and murderer from the beginning; but God will do what will inure to your benefit in the long run and permanently, whiles in the interim it may seem uncomfortable.

THIS WAS WHAT SAUL DID, WHO WENT AGAINST THE WILL OF GOD — THAT HIS REBELLION IS THE SAME AS WITCHCRAFT AND IDOLATRY.
