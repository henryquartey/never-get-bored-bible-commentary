1These are the journeys of the children of Israel, which went forth out of the land of Egypt with their armies under the hand of Moses and Aaron.

2And Moses wrote their goings out according to their journeys by the commandment of the LORD: and these are their journeys according to their goings out.

3And they departed from Rameses in the first month, on the fifteenth day of the first month; on the morrow after the passover the children of Israel went out with an high hand in the sight of all the Egyptians.

4For the Egyptians buried all their firstborn, which the LORD had smitten among them: upon their gods also the LORD executed judgments.

NII OKAI · Numbers 33:1222 April 2024

Oh my oh my... All these movements were by the Spirit of God; the cloud 🌥 directed where they were to camp and for how long.

And not only that, but the Lord asked for Moses to specifically record these journeys, journeys spanning 40 years.

Now come to think of it, did God follow his initial script as intended, or was it influenced and adjusted based on the preparedness of Israel 🇮🇱 to obey him and how willing they were to follow his commands, judgements and statutes?

Oh, and many times Israel failed, prolonging their camping 🏕, causing change in directions and eventually leading to 38 extra years of wandering, till they were all dead and their children taking their place.

I wish I saw the places and the pattern these journeys formed and the meaning thereof.

But alas, the scriptures do not tell us.

NII OKAI · Numbers 33:3622 April 2024

I'm wondering how much preparing had to go into each journey, the scouts that had to be sent ahead, the setting up and setting down 👇.

The studying of the terrains. Moses at a point required and pleaded with the son of Jethro to be with them, as he knew the wilderness better.

One would have thought 💭 that for a man 👨 who spoke to God face to face, it was just a matter of asking God.

But I believe that God was not at the beck and call of Moses, but he had to wait for long in prayer just like we all.

The only difference being God appeared in person to answer him rather than in shadows and patterns — dreams, impressions etc.

So he had to rely on his common sense, delegations and counsel from others to successfully lead his people.

This having to wait on God before he came is seen subtly in many places. For instance, the children of Israel went three days without finding water and were ready to stone Moses before God showed up.

If Moses could have obtained instant answers from God, why else would he wait until the people were dying from thirst, the weak and elderly convulsing and being feverish, before praying to the Lord?

Moses could not just tell God: where is the nearest water source, the best place to get bread, and the next step to take to receive the miracle of meat 🥩, ahead of the people being hungry and angry 😡.

Moses just could not do that, as God was not that available.

He was visible, he could be seen and felt, but he was not available until he wanted to talk.

See how many days and nights Moses spent in the mount to receive the 10 Commandments — 40 days and nights 🌃.

To preserve their own posterity, they needed to drive out the inhabitants.

NII OKAI · Numbers 33:559 March 2026

Their destiny was in their own hands; indeed, the extent of their own greatness was in the volume of appetite they could muster.

God gave them the land, and they had the power to drive all out or some out.

Obviously, if we are given the same opportunity, our response will be: we will take all that belongs to us. But it is not that simple.

Yes, God was with them to win every battle, but it was still going to be a battle.

And most hate battles and the work that goes into conquest. Most people lack the requisite appetite for mass-scale victory and all-round success.

God's best for them was to conquer all, but they fell short of that. May God give us the grace, and may we have sufficient drive, energy and willingness to conquer all.

56Moreover it shall come to pass, that I shall do unto you, as I thought to do unto them.

NII OKAI · Numbers 33:569 March 2022

Simple: God needed his agenda to be fulfilled, and Israel 🇮🇱 was fulfilling that agenda.

The agenda was cleansing, cleansing the land of the filth of the people.

Later, when Israel became filthy, they were also cleaned off the land by another people.

So in our world today, God will cleanse this world of the wickedness and moral filth before the great day of our Lord.
