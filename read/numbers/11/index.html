NII OKAI · Numbers 11:2515 April 2024

A few key 🗝 things to note 🗒:

1. God came down 👇 into the cloud 🌥.

2. God took of the spirit upon Moses.

3. The spirit upon Moses was given to Moses by God; in other words, the spirit of God was upon Moses.

4. That same spirit is now given among the 70, with the evidence of their prophesying.

5. The same spirit is not divided into seventy parts just because he is upon 70 people, but that spirit is omnipresent and thus can rest on 70 people at a time.

6. The one spirit gives them utterance in prophecy.

7. God, who is spirit and gives his spirit to be upon Moses, at that same time speaks to Moses, and Moses speaks back to him.

8. This is much like Jesus in Jordan during his baptism: the spirit descends upon Jesus and God speaks to Jesus.

9. In point 7 we see a duality, whiles in 8 we see a trinity.

10. Even though God's spirit is upon Moses and he teaches Moses the voice of God, justice, judgements, discernment etc., Moses recognizes that there is one God, and the fact of God's spirit upon him does not produce another God.

11. The spirit is an extension of the many possibilities, powers and/or attributes of God, so he continues in his conversation with God.

12. Same with Jesus, who often refers to, talks to and prays to the Father, though many times he also teaches about the spirit of the Father.

God is one, and he is Father, Son and Holy Spirit, but in the Old Testament he is Father and Spirit.

Another quick thought 💭.

God is spirit, so he is omnipresent, and even his manifest presence, so called, can be at many places; indeed his manifest presence is omnipresent, or better put, can be omnipresent if he chooses to.
