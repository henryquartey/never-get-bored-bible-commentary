NII OKAI · Numbers 16:1418 April 2024

Moses was put in a very difficult situation.

Indeed the words of these men carry a semblance of truth.

They were not in the promised land and they knew they were going to die in the wilderness,

all because of their rebellion when the opportunity was ripe.

They had brought it upon themselves, and they were only blaming Moses for it.

They were Levites; they should have taken it up with the Lord.

Moses was the front for God, and God had really put him in a difficult situation.

There was no reason to listen to Moses, because now the reason for following him all this while was gone.

How was Moses to govern them? I can imagine the rumors and insults behind his back.

And we can sense the conversations they had had in time past from the response of these two men.

Now they had grown so stubborn they told Moses to his face the things they spoke of in his absence..

Moses often pleaded with God for the people, and in this case, though not recorded, I'm sure he went to God time and again to reconsider his decision, or at least reduce the 40 years.

And time and again he refused.

The other thing is, so long as God refused, Moses had to live with reproach like this.

I'm sure he had heard such statements, but it hadn't blown up like this. It was only a matter of time.

And now here we are... Open rebellion, towards God and the man of God, Moses.

29If these men die the common death of all men, or if they be visited after the visitation of all men; then the LORD hath not sent me.

30But if the LORD make a new thing, and the earth open her mouth, and swallow them up, with all that appertain unto them, and they go down quick into the pit; then ye shall understand that these men have provoked the LORD.

35And there came out a fire from the LORD, and consumed the two hundred and fifty men that offered incense.

37Speak unto Eleazar the son of Aaron the priest, that he take up the censers out of the burning, and scatter thou the fire yonder; for they are hallowed.

38The censers of these sinners against their own souls, let them make them broad plates for a covering of the altar: for they offered them before the LORD, therefore they are hallowed: and they shall be a sign unto the children of Israel.
