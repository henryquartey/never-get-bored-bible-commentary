1And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying,

2Make thee two trumpets of silver; of a whole piece shalt thou make them: that thou mayest use them for the calling of the assembly, and for the journeying of the camps.

3And when they shall blow with them, all the assembly shall assemble themselves to thee at the door of the tabernacle of the congregation.

4And if they blow but with one trumpet, then the princes, which are heads of the thousands of Israel, shall gather themselves unto thee.

5When ye blow an alarm, then the camps that lie on the east parts shall go forward.

6When ye blow an alarm the second time, then the camps that lie on the south side shall take their journey: they shall blow an alarm for their journeys.

7But when the congregation is to be gathered together, ye shall blow, but ye shall not sound an alarm.

8And the sons of Aaron, the priests, shall blow with the trumpets; and they shall be to you for an ordinance for ever throughout your generations.

9And if ye go to war in your land against the enemy that oppresseth you, then ye shall blow an alarm with the trumpets; and ye shall be remembered before the LORD your God, and ye shall be saved from your enemies.

NII OKAI · Numbers 10:92 March 2026

Interesting ritual. So you see the children of Israel blowing the trumpet during war and you presume it is a war cry, or to charge the people, but little do you know that it is a call.

It is a call to their God. And now suddenly the battle turns in their favour.

This reminds me of our traditional priests who dance during their rituals, shake some cowries here and there. Only they know the exact code or mystery of the gods they are communicating with.

This makes me wonder: if another person follows the exact same routine after careful study but does not know what it means, would they yield the same results?

I don't know. Maybe, maybe not. Remember the gods are living, intelligent beings. Maybe they may choose to overlook the lack of knowledge and show up, or maybe, like any machine, once you follow the process and press any button it will respond as expected, even if you don't know what it was for. Once more, I don't know.

NII OKAI · Numbers 10:3513 April 2024

Was this expression a mere chant and slogan, or was it a reflection of reality?

Who were the enemies of God at this time?

Israel had had no wars, no attempted taking over of any nation, for them to have enemies.

They had left Egypt 🇪🇬, who was their chief enemy. So now in the wilderness, who was their enemy, for Moses to say at the rising of the Lord that his enemies be scattered?

The question 🙋 actually is: what accounted for God moving when he did?

What was he seeing or foreseeing that made him cause all of Israel 🇮🇱 to change locations?

Knowing God for who he is, there was intelligence and wisdom and design behind his movements.

They were not erratic. God, I believe, moved to test Israel 🇮🇱, to try them, and to keep them safe from invading enemies or plans of the enemy to invade these wanderers with no place they could call home 🏡.

It is actually possible, and more likely, that God moved to both save Israel from enemy nations who had heard of Israel's goal of returning to their land and destroying all current occupants.

Of course no nation would sit aloof; some might attempt to invade them. Though 💭 the fear 😱 of God was upon the nations round about, so they mostly dared not attack.

Who attacks a nation in whom God is visibly present and who had wrecked the mighty Egypt?

Also, in God's moving he could be saving Israel from invaders and at the same time testing their loyalty with lack, or even making provision for them in a miraculous way.

Indeed the tests of God are actually not tests of lack but tests of his provisions.

What initially seems like lack was actually divine provision and abundance. Just that the process that led to the provision involves suffering some lack for a while.
