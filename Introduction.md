# Never Get Bored
### Nii Okai's Journal Through Scripture

> *"Take a walk with me as we journey through Scripture — and understand every passage in a way you never imagined or thought possible. You will never get bored."*

---

## Introduction

If you have ever found the Bible boring, this journal is for you.

I know the feeling. So many people open the Bible expecting to be bored — the genealogies, the laws, the "thou shalts," chapter after chapter — and they quietly close it again. For over two decades I have been reading these same Scriptures, and I have found the very opposite: a book breathtaking in its wisdom, its humanity, its drama, and its God. What you hold here is my attempt to pull back the dull-looking surface and let you see what I have seen.

These pages are not a commentary in the academic sense. I am not a scholar handing down conclusions from a height. I am a fellow traveller scribbling in the margins as I walk — and I have been walking, and writing, for more than twenty years. What began as private notes between me and God slowly became this: a verse-by-verse record of what puzzled me, what stirred me, what made me laugh, what broke me, and what I never quite figured out.

So you will find questions I never fully answered. You will find me arguing with the text, wondering aloud, catching a small detail I had read a hundred times and never noticed. You will find my own struggles — with doubt, with sin, with the long silences of God — sitting right next to the verses that met me in them. That is the point. This is a journal, not a lecture.

A few honest words before you begin. Because these notes span twenty years, you will catch me growing, changing my mind, even contradicting an earlier version of myself. There are typos. There are half-formed thoughts. I have left them mostly as they were written, because that is what a journal is — and because I would rather be real than polished. If you find an error in my doctrine, or a verse I have taken out of context, I genuinely welcome the correction. The Word of God is eternal and infallible; it is the man writing about it who is not.

So read these beside your own Bible, never instead of it. Let them provoke you, challenge you, and send you straight back to the text to see for yourself. And if even one note makes you lean in closer to a passage you would have skimmed — if even once you catch yourself thinking, *"I never saw that before"* — then this journal has done exactly what I hoped.

The Bible came alive for me. I believe it can come alive for you too.

Welcome to the journey. I'm glad you're here.

**— Nii Okai**
